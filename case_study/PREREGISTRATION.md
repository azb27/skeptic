# Gold Sniper Replay Harness — Pre-Registration

**Locked:** 25 September 2026, before any harness result exists.
**Baseline under test:** `gold_sniper_v5_3.pine` (1,359 lines), as deployed.
**Live evidence to date:** 25 trades over 6 sessions (17, 18, 21, 22, 23, 24 September 2026), 10 reached TP1 (40%), −5.15R at 1:1.

The purpose of this document is to fix the hypotheses, the metrics, the decision rules and the stopping conditions *before* results are visible, so that a favourable number cannot be manufactured by choosing the test after the fact. Anything not written here is exploratory and cannot, on its own, justify a change to the live script.

---

## 0. Harness acceptance test (gate — must pass before any hypothesis is reported)

The harness is a re-implementation of v5.3 in Python. It is only useful if it reproduces the script. Before any hypothesis result is quoted:

| Check | Threshold |
|---|---|
| Signals reproduced on a broker-matched feed: same bar, same direction, same path (ICC/CONT/REV) | ≥ 23 of the 25 live signals |
| Entry, SL, TP1–TP3 levels on reproduced signals | within $0.05 |
| ADX at logged bars vs Pine's ADX | mean absolute error ≤ 0.5 |
| Choppiness Index at logged bars | mean absolute error ≤ 1.0 |
| Rejected-candidate blocking reasons reproduced | ≥ 90% exact match |

Any discrepancy above these thresholds is a harness bug and is fixed before proceeding. **A mismatch is never explained away as "the feed differs" without demonstrating that on a matched feed it disappears.**

Failure mode this gate exists to prevent: a harness that is subtly wrong produces confident numbers about a system that does not exist.

## 1. Metrics and conventions (fixed)

- **Primary metric:** expectancy per trade in R, closing the whole position at TP1 (1:1). R = entry-to-stop distance at signal time.
- **Secondary metric:** expectancy with thirds at TP1/TP2/TP3 and stop to break-even after TP1 (the deployed behaviour).
- **Hit rate:** fraction of trades touching TP1 before the stop.
- **Trade life:** 41 bars, matching the live timeout.
- **Ambiguous bars** (one 5-minute bar spans both a target and the in-force stop): resolved **pessimistically** (stop first) for the primary metric. The optimistic figure is reported alongside; any conclusion that flips between the two is reported as unresolved, not as a result.
- **Costs:** results are reported at three round-trip spreads — **$0.20, $0.50, $0.90**. The $0.50 column is the one that decides. (The real broker spread is still unmeasured; this is a sensitivity band, not a guess dressed as data.)
- **Evaluation blocks:** whole sessions. Per-trade statistics are reported with session-clustered uncertainty, because many trades on one trending day are not independent evidence.

## 2. Data and splits (fixed)

- **In-sample (IS):** the older 70% of available 5-minute history, by date.
- **Out-of-sample (OS):** the most recent 30%, **not examined until an IS decision is recorded in this file's changelog.**
- **Live sessions (17–24 Sep 2026):** reserved for the fidelity gate in §0. They are *not* a performance test — they are already contaminated, since every hypothesis here was generated by looking at them.
- Feed differences (COMEX futures vs broker spot) are documented per run. Cross-feed results are directional evidence, never a promotion criterion.

## 3. The three hypotheses

### H1 — Re-entry after a stop-out

**Origin:** live sample, found post hoc on 24 Sep. 5 of 5 re-entries reached TP1 versus 5 of 20 fresh signals (Fisher p = 0.005). Stable for re-entry windows from 60 to 180 minutes.

**Definition (locked):** a signal is a *re-entry* if it is in the same direction as the immediately preceding trade, that trade exited at its stop (SL or break-even), the new signal fires within **90 minutes** of that exit, and no opposite-direction trade intervened. Everything else is a *fresh* signal.

**Prediction:** re-entry TP1 hit rate exceeds fresh TP1 hit rate by **≥ 10 percentage points**, and re-entry expectancy exceeds fresh expectancy by **≥ 0.15R**.

**Minimum sample:** 30 re-entry trades. Below that, the result is "insufficient data" regardless of how good it looks.

**Candidate change if it passes:** relax cooldown and flip constraints for same-direction re-entries only, or add an explicit re-entry path with its own threshold. The specific variant is chosen *after* the effect is confirmed, and is itself re-tested out-of-sample.

**Pre-committed kill condition:** if re-entry expectancy is within 0.15R of fresh, H1 is dead and is not revived by redefining the window.

### H2 — ADX floor for continuation signals

**Origin:** live evidence, two-sided and decaying. Removing the floor was +4.0R ahead after 3 sessions, +1.15R after 6. Among trades actually taken, winners had higher ADX than losers (30.0 vs 25.3).

**Variants (locked, 7 runs):** floor ∈ {none, 15, 18, **20 = live**, 23, 25}, plus **"floor with expansion override"**: the floor is bypassed when the signal bar's true range ≥ 1.5× the prior bar's ATR.

**Prediction:** registered as a **null**. No variant beats the live setting of 20 by ≥ 0.10R per trade.

**Promotion rule (all four required):**
1. beats floor = 20 by ≥ 0.10R per trade in-sample;
2. holds ≥ 0.05R per trade out-of-sample;
3. **neighbouring parameter values also improve** — a lone spike surrounded by worse values is treated as noise, not a setting;
4. survives the $0.50 spread column.

### H3 — Killzone windows

**Origin:** the 14:00–16:30 UAE gap has produced visible moves since the first session. First measured on 23 Sep (+3R better on that day), reversed on 24 Sep (−3R). Net zero over two days.

**Variants (locked, 4 runs):** (a) current windows; (b) current + 14:00–16:30 UAE; (c) 24-hour, off-window penalty removed; (d) current windows with the off-window penalty halved (conviction −0.75, threshold +0.75).

**Prediction:** registered as a **null**. No variant beats the current windows by ≥ 0.10R per trade.

**Promotion rule:** same four conditions as H2.

## 4. Multiple testing

Total pre-registered runs: **1 (H1) + 7 (H2) + 4 (H3) = 12**. Every additional variant run for any reason is appended to the changelog below with its result, whether or not it is reported. The trial count enters the significance assessment; a nominal p-value is not quoted without it.

Family-wise α = 0.05 across the three hypotheses → **per-hypothesis α = 0.0167**.

Exploratory analyses are permitted and encouraged, but are labelled exploratory and generate *new* pre-registrations rather than promotions.

## 5. Stopping rule

If none of H1–H3 passes its promotion rule:

- v5.3 stays frozen;
- no further threshold tuning is undertaken on this instrument;
- the project moves to the instrument question (measure the real broker spread, and compare the same timestamped hypothesis on an exchange-traded gold reference).

"Keep tuning until something passes" is not an available option, because with enough variants something always passes.
